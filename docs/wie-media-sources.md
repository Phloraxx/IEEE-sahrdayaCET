# WIE public media sources

This file records the approved provenance of media used by the public WIE page.

## Official WIE identity

- `public/images/wie/ieee-wie-official-background.webp`
- `public/images/wie/ieee-wie-official-banner.webp`

Both are responsive derivatives of the official IEEE Women in Engineering
virtual-background artwork supplied through the IEEE WIE visual identity
resources. The IEEE and WIE marks must not be redrawn, recoloured, stretched,
or separated from the approved composition.

## Sahrdaya event photography

- `wie-community.webp` derives from the original WiTech 2026 photograph
  `IMG_20260711_103019093_HDR.jpg` in the WIE Drive archive.
- `witech-feature.webp` derives from the original WiTech 2026 photograph
  `IMG_20260711_104848155.jpg` in the WIE Drive archive.

The source photographs are 4096 × 3072 originals. The WebP derivatives remove
camera metadata and are sized for public web delivery.

## Historical activity artwork

The earlier 2024–2025 images extracted from combined reports were highly
compressed and are intentionally not published. Those records use the designed
WIE activity fallback in the application until an original poster or photograph
is approved and uploaded.

Do not restore report screenshots merely to fill an image slot. Prefer, in
order: an approved original photograph, an original event poster, then the
consistent designed fallback.


## Poster and design-source audit

The connected Figma file `IEEE SAHRDAYA SB'26` was reviewed. It contains the
Student Branch and society logo library, including WIE black and white logo
variants, but no WIE event-poster frames.

Dedicated Google Drive folders named `Poster` were also checked for Beyond
Business, the Gen AI workshop and Pioneering Safe Cyberspace. Those folders are
currently empty. The designed WIE fallback remains intentional for those events
until an approved original poster or photograph is supplied.

The public page must not use `SBA65601`. That value had no supporting source in
Drive, PocketBase, Figma or an approved IEEE record and was removed from the
page.
