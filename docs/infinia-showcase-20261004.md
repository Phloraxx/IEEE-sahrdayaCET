# Infinia showcase — source and design record

The user requested a distinct Infinia experience, an animated rainbow INFINIA navigation label and removal of the mixed flagship timeline/lower promotional sections. Scope is public editorial UI on staging; no operational records or email pathways change.

## Source hierarchy and figures

Completed Infinia 2.0 report: [INFINIA REPORT.docx](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1PWrbyYmkbHfnzIr83N4nUWUCKYJCVllc/view). Its opening outcome confirms 400+ participants, 70 volunteers, 15 professional speakers and 10+ industry collaborations for 26–28 September 2025. These are historical report figures, not live counters. Repeated 419 session figures are not summed.
The six workshop attendance figures are AEGIS 63, UIXOR 61, HOVERX 52, QBIT 40, IONIX 53 and AXIOM 30. These are labelled per-session attendance and never added to the event total. The report supplies workshop narratives, panel affiliations, expos, healthcare and wearable-tech content.

[Annual Report 2025](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1OCLVCE56xV_QSX8oMqkqSw_hX6I0ERnYvmBhnkiqWNY) supplies Vitals 24: 30–31 August 2025, 724 individual registrations, 270+ teams, 150+ colleges and 12 states, with 12 teams shortlisted for the offline final. These belong to the pre-event and remain separate from Infinia attendance.
2024 dates, nine tracks and narratives retain the source hierarchy in [the existing archive record](flagship-stories-20261003.md). Conflicting 2024 participant totals remain unpublished.
Public professional names/affiliations are historical 2025 session credits. Neither awards nor future editions are inferred.

## Routing, performance and acceptance

Canonical SSR URLs: /infinia, /infinia/2025 and /infinia/2024. /flagships and /flagships/infinia redirect 301 to /infinia; valid legacy Infinia edition paths redirect 301 to their new leaf. Unknown editions return 404. Altair parent/2023/2022 pages remain independently crawlable. Production sitemap lists canonical routes, while staging retains noindex.

No PocketBase dependency, schema, registration, payment, notification or mail mutation is introduced. Fonts remain bundled. Hero community photo is eager, other imagery lazy, intrinsic dimensions reserve space, video fetch waits for play. Critical copy and edition links are SSR; poster/photo viewer supports keyboard focus, Escape and focus return. Native image links remain usable without JavaScript; the document’s shared skip link targets the main landmark.
